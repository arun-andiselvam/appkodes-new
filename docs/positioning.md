## 1. Positioning in one line

**Appkodes builds custom software and mobile apps on a fixed price and a fixed launch date, and sells ready-to-use business software through the Appkodes Store.**

### Positioning statement

For founders, startups and growing businesses who need an app built without the usual delays and budget overruns, Appkodes is a software and mobile app development company that agrees the price and the launch date before work starts, and gets most apps live in about 30 days. We can promise this because we have built these kinds of apps many times, agree the scope in writing before we start, and keep the whole build with one in-house team. Every app is a new build for its client; we never sell or resell ready-made software. For businesses that just need working software today, the Appkodes Store sells ready-made business apps they can register for and buy directly.

## 5. Target audience

> Segments below are the working assumption. Geography is still open (see Open decisions).

### 5.1 Founders and startups (Services + Solutions)

- **Who:** First-time and repeat founders launching an app-based business — marketplace, delivery, streaming, dating, booking.
- **What they want:** A launched app they can take to users and investors, fast.
- **What they fear:** Agencies that miss dates, add costs halfway through, or disappear after launch.
- **What convinces them:** A fixed price and date in writing, case studies of similar apps, and seeing apps we have shipped of the same kind.
- **Entry points:** Solutions menu, MVP and Startup pages, blog guides on cost and planning.

### 5.2 Small and mid-sized businesses (Services)

- **Who:** Established businesses adding an app to what they already do — a restaurant group, a clinic chain, a retailer with a Shopify store, a logistics company.
- **What they want:** An app that fits how they already work, which their own team can run.
- **What they fear:** Paying for something their staff can't use or maintain.
- **What convinces them:** Industry-specific pages, clear handover and support, examples from their sector.
- **Entry points:** Services menu by industry (Delivery, Healthcare, Buy and Sell).

### 5.3 Media and content businesses (Solutions)

- **Who:** Small content studios, regional producers and creators building streaming, short video or live apps.
- **What they want:** Their own platform instead of depending on YouTube or other big platforms.
- **What they fear:** High cost and technical complexity of video.
- **What convinces them:** Streaming apps we have shipped, clear pricing ranges, proof our builds handle scale.
- **Entry points:** Entertainment group, OTT Streaming, Live Streaming, Short Video pages.

### 5.4 Operations-focused small businesses (Store)

- **Who:** Small teams — contractors, service businesses, shops, consultants — who need scheduling, project or inventory software.
- **What they want:** Software that works today at a fair price.
- **What they fear:** Long setup, hidden pricing, getting stuck with a tool nobody supports.
- **What convinces them:** Clear public pricing, a demo or trial, simple onboarding, visible support.
- **Entry points:** Store, category searches such as "construction management software".

### Who we are not for

- Buyers looking for the cheapest possible script to download and run themselves.
- Large enterprises needing formal procurement, custom SLAs and long compliance processes.
- AI-first projects (refer to Hitasoft).

## 6. Core promise and proof

### The promise

**Your app, live in 30 days. A fixed price and a fixed launch date, agreed before we start.**

### Why we can keep it

1. **We have built this kind of app many times.** We know the screens, the edge cases and the store rules before we start, so nothing is learned on the client's time.
2. **Price and date are agreed up front** in the free costed plan, not estimated and revised later.
3. **An in-house team** builds, launches and supports every app. No outsourcing chain.

### Proof points (use consistently across the site)

| Proof | Where it appears | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1000+ businesses served | Services menu panel, homepage | Keep the number identical everywhere |
| 18 yrs shipping apps | Services menu panel, homepage | **Verify wording** — if this includes Hitasoft's history, the sentence must stay accurate for Appkodes |
| Case studies | "See case studies" CTA, Solutions pages | Real apps with the client's permission |
| Blog written from real projects | Resources | "Notes from the build" |

> **Important:** "30 days" must be qualified on detail pages (e.g. "most first releases go live in about 30 days; the costed plan sets the exact scope and date"). Heavier builds such as OTT or super apps may take longer. Never let the promise be broken by the smallest print on the page.

## 9. Voice and language

### Voice

- **Plain.** Short sentences. Everyday words. Say what the thing does.
- **Concrete.** Numbers, dates and examples beat adjectives. "Live in 30 days" beats "lightning-fast delivery".
- **Confident, not loud.** No hype words, no exclamation marks in headlines.
- **Honest.** If something takes longer or costs more, say so on the page.

### Words to avoid (banned in site copy)

| Avoid | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| clone, clone script | The positioning we're leaving | app, platform, solution |
| open source | Misrepresents what the buyer gets | your own app, full ownership (only where accurate) |
| IT services | Generic; makes us sound like an outsourcing vendor | software development, app development |
| AI development (as an offer) | Belongs to Hitasoft | — |
| script / ready-made script / ready-made solution (for Services and Solutions) | Old business model; we never sell ready-made software | custom app, your app, app type |
| Enterprise | Sets procurement and SLA expectations we don't target | business, growing business |
| world-class, cutting-edge, seamless, robust, revolutionary | Empty claims | a specific fact or number |

### Rules for brand references ("like Uber")

- **Allowed:** short menu subtitles and in-page comparison sections ("How it compares to…"), FAQs.
- **Not allowed:** page headlines (H1), product names, page titles, URL slugs, meta titles, logos or brand imagery.
- **Always:** describe the product by what it does first. The brand reference is a hint, not the identity.

### Words we use

fixed price · fixed date · costed plan · live in 30 days · your app · built by our team · apps we've shipped · run and grow · case studies · business software (Store)

## 11. Page-level messaging guide

### Homepage

- **H1:** Your app, live in 30 days.
- **Subhead:** Most software projects run late and over budget. Ours ship on a fixed price and a fixed date. You get an app your team can run and grow.
- **CTAs:** Get a free costed plan · See case studies
- **Sections, in order:** proof bar (1000+ businesses, years shipping) → how the fixed price and date work → Solutions preview → industries → case studies → Store teaser → Resources → final CTA.

### Service pages (e.g. MVP Software, Food Delivery)

- Lead with the outcome for that buyer, not the technology.
- Show the fixed-price, fixed-date process in 3–4 steps.
- Link to the matching Solutions and case studies.
- End with: Get a free costed plan.

### Solution pages (e.g. Taxi Booking)

- **H1** describes what it does ("Ride-hailing app with live driver tracking"), not the brand it resembles.
- Explain that **their** app is designed and built new for their business, by a team that has built this app type before.
- Show features, apps included (rider, driver, admin), examples of apps we have shipped, typical timeline and price range.
- Brand comparison allowed lower on the page or in FAQs only.
- End with: Get a free costed plan.

### Store pages

- Lead with the job the software does ("Let clients book time with you without back-and-forth emails").
- Show public pricing, what's included, how to get started and support.
- Include the cross-link to Services: "Need it changed for your business? Get a free costed plan."

### Resources / blog

- Written from real projects ("Notes from the build").
- Every post links to one relevant Service or Solution and ends with a soft CTA.

## 12. SEO principles tied to positioning

1. **One page per topic.** If a topic appears in both Services and Solutions menus (for example Taxi Booking, Live Streaming, Short Video), both menu links must point to the **same URL**, or the two pages must target clearly different searches. Two separate pages chasing the same keyword compete with each other.
2. **Target what the buyer searches, described by function:** "taxi booking app development", "OTT platform development", "construction management software". Not "X clone".
3. **Redirect every retired clone-script URL** (301) to its closest new page. Never let old ranking pages 404.
4. **Page titles and meta descriptions** follow the same language rules as page copy (Section 9).
5. **Store pages target category keywords** ("mobile inventory app for small business"), which are a separate intent from development keywords — keep the two apart.
6. **Watch Search Console for 8–12 weeks after launch** and fix drops page by page.
