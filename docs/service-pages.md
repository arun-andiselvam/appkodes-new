## 4. Content Guidelines & Copy Strategy

### The Agitation (Hero Section)
* **Focus:** The danger of "over-engineering" an AI idea without testing it first.
* **Angle:** Do not spend your entire budget building a custom platform only to find out the core AI logic does not resonate with users. Build a prototype, put it in closed testing, and let the data guide the final build.

### Comparison Table: Rapid Prototyping vs. Full-Scale Development
* **Focus:** Highlight the risk mitigation and speed of a prototype.
* **Metrics to compare:** Timeline (2-4 Weeks vs. 4-6 Months), Primary Goal (Validating core logic vs. Scaling for thousands of users), Architecture (Lean API wrappers vs. Heavy cloud infrastructure), and Cost (Fractional fixed-price vs. Massive capital expenditure).

### Concrete Use Cases (Staggered Layout)
* **Use Case 1: Fintech & Shared Ledger MVPs.** Describe prototyping a core expense categorization and dynamic debt calculation engine. By building a lean web interface, a startup can validate the financial math and multi-user logic with a small closed testing group before investing in native mobile development.
* **Use Case 2: Inventory System Automation.** Highlight testing an MVP for a retail electronics inventory platform, where a simple UI allows warehouse staff to test a new vision-based AI scanner on actual stock before integrating it deeply into the main commercial database.
* **Use Case 3: Community App Moderation.** Detail a proof of concept for an automated social feed within a community platform. The prototype tests the AI's ability to handle media permissions and correctly filter out inappropriate content based on strict community rules.

### Frequently Asked Questions
* **Q1: Is the prototype code "throwaway," or can we build upon it?** (Explain that while the UI might be a rapid mock-up, the core AI API wrappers and prompt engineering logic are highly structured and transferrable to the final production build).
* **Q2: Can we launch a rapid prototype to the Google Play Store?** (Clarify that while prototypes are perfect for internal validation or formal closed testing groups (like a 12-tester beta), deploying to public app stores usually requires the full compliance and Data Safety reviews of a production build).
* **Q3: What do I need to bring to the table to start a prototype?** (Note that they only need a clear business problem and access to the sample data the AI will need to process).
